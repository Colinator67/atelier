Questions

Will it work with my machine?

SVG, DXF and PDF files open in LightBurn, xTool Creative Space, Glowforge, LaserGRBL and most laser software. STL and 3MF files work with every slicer: Bambu Studio, PrusaSlicer, OrcaSlicer, Cura. Tell me which machine you have and I'll size the file for its bed.

Can I sell what I make with the file?

Yes, you can sell the pieces you cut or print. The file itself is for your own use: please don't resell or share it.

How long does it take?

I reply within 24 to 48 hours with a first preview or my questions. Design time depends on complexity and is stated with the preview, before you commit to anything.

I'm not in France. Is that a problem?

Not at all: files are delivered by email, anywhere in the world. I'm based in Strasbourg, France, and I'm happy to work in English.

Can you make the piece too?

Yes. I can laser cut or 3D print it and ship it from France. Mention it in your request and the shipping cost will be on the quote. The French site has instant quotes for printing and cutting.
